Got a warning notice in the mail?

Every LA speed camera starts with a 60-day warning period. During it, drivers going 11 mph or more over the limit get a $0 warning notice instead of a ticket.

After that, notices become fines: $50 for 11–15 mph over, rising to as much as $500 for extreme speeding. Fines are civil penalties mailed to the vehicle's registered owner, and the city offers reduced payment options for low-income drivers.

Source and official details: LADOT Speed Safety System.
