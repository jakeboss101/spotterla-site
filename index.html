SpotterLA

Heads-up alerts for LA speed cameras.

SpotterLA warns you with a chime and a voice alert as you approach a Los Angeles speed camera, even while the app is closed.

Works in the background. Set it once and drive. No need to keep the app open.

Private by design. Your location is processed only on your iPhone and never leaves it. No ads, no tracking, no account.

$9.99 a year after a 7-day free trial. That's less than $1 a month, and one speed camera ticket costs $50+.

Always obey posted speed limits and don't handle your phone while driving. Camera locations come from LADOT and may be incomplete or out of date.
