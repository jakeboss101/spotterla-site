    Got a warning notice in the mail?

    Every LA speed camera starts with a 60-day warning period. During it, drivers going 11 mph or more over the limit get a $0 warning notice instead of a ticket.

    After that, notices become fines: $50 for 11–15 mph over, rising to as much as $500 for extreme speeding. Fines are civil penalties mailed to the vehicle's registered owner, and the city offers reduced payment options for low-income drivers.

    Source and official details: LADOT Speed Safety System.

    Questions

    Does SpotterLA work outside Los Angeles?

    Not yet. It covers the City of Los Angeles speed camera program run by LADOT.

    Do I need to keep the app open?

    No. Turn on Camera Alerts, set location to “Always,” and SpotterLA works even when the app is closed.

    Will it drain my battery?

    Not much. GPS turns on only while you're driving near cameras and turns off a few minutes after you park. The rest of the time, SpotterLA uses almost no battery.

    Is my location shared with anyone?

    No. Your location never leaves your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

    What happens when my free trial ends?

    If you keep auto-renew on, your $9.99 yearly plan starts and alerts keep working. If you turn it off, we'll remind you the day before the trial ends, and alerts pause after it does.

    Is this about dodging tickets?

    No. A lot of these cameras are near schools, and we built SpotterLA because a quick heads-up gets people to slow down right where it matters. The locations are public, published by the City of LA. Please drive the speed limit.

    Where do the speed limits come from?

    From OpenStreetMap. If a camera's limit isn't clear, SpotterLA just says “Speed camera. Approaching.” Always go by the posted sign.

    Always obey posted speed limits and don't handle your phone while driving. Camera locations come from LADOT and may be incomplete or out of date. Speed limits come from OpenStreetMap and may be missing or wrong, so always follow posted signs. SpotterLA is not affiliated with the City of Los Angeles.
