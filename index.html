Questions

Does SpotterLA work outside Los Angeles?

Not yet. It covers the City of Los Angeles speed camera program run by LADOT.

Do I need to keep the app open?

No. Once protection is on, SpotterLA works in the background. Just set location access to “Always.”

Will it drain my battery?

SpotterLA uses Apple's geofencing, which wakes the app only near cameras. It never keeps GPS running.

Is my location shared with anyone?

No. Your location never leaves your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

What happens when my free trial ends?

If you keep auto-renew on, your $9.99 yearly plan starts and alerts keep working. If you turn it off, we'll remind you the day before the trial ends, and alerts pause after it does.

Is this about dodging tickets?

SpotterLA uses public camera locations published by the City of Los Angeles. It's a reminder to check your speed, not a way to avoid enforcement. Always obey posted limits.

Always obey posted speed limits and don't handle your phone while driving. Camera locations come from LADOT and may be incomplete or out of date. SpotterLA is not affiliated with the City of Los Angeles.
