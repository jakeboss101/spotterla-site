SpotterLA

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

The SpotterLA app doesn't collect or share any personal data. There are no ads, analytics, tracking, or accounts.

Location

SpotterLA uses your location on your iPhone to alert you near speed cameras, including in the background. Your location never leaves your device. You can turn off location access anytime in Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Data on your device

A few settings are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Android test sign-up

If you sign up for the Android test on this website, we keep the email you enter only to add you as a tester and send you the download link. It's stored in a Google Form we own and is never shared or sold. Email us to have it deleted.

Contact

Email support@spotterla.com.

Always obey posted speed limits. Camera data may be out of date. Not affiliated with any government agency.
