Alerts aren't working while the app is closed

Open Settings › SpotterLA › Location and choose Always, with Precise Location turned on. Make sure notifications are allowed too.

Managing or canceling your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. In SpotterLA, open the settings menu and tap Manage Subscription, or go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Restoring a purchase on a new iPhone

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open SpotterLA's settings menu, and tap Restore Purchases.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
